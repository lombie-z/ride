# creature.glb

Made from scratch for this project by art/build-creature.py (Blender headless): the mesh, rig and `Run` animation are all generated by that script. No third-party mesh, rig, animation or texture is in the file, so there is no source licence or attribution to carry.

Looked at and not used: Quaternius's CC0 "Wolf" (https://poly.pizza/m/P1gU3Qkr9r, https://poly.pizza/m/XU7oNeKShV). It has a real gallop clip, but it's a chunky 2k tri cartoon wolf on a 51 bone rig, so the mesh would have been replaced anyway and the clip wouldn't fit the long, low body. It was only watched as a check on gallop timing; nothing was copied from it.

Feel taken from the specimens in art/reference/concept-core-lab.png (the project's own concept art). Not modelled on any existing game or film creature.

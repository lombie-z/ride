# Audio licences

Every file here is either synthesised for this project (numpy/scipy, no third-party audio) or built from CC0 recordings on OpenGameArt. The licence shown on each source page was checked on 2026-11-04; each page lists CC0 only. OpenGameArt's CC0 label is the uploader's statement and hasn't been traced further.

All files: gain set to the target loudness, encoded as Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz.

## Built on CC0 recordings

### rain-loop
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-2 (amb_rain2.flac)
- Author: Kresiek The Furry. Licence: CC0.
- Changed: cut 19 s starting at 539 s (the steadiest stretch by short-term level), 1.5 s crossfade to loop, resampled from 48 kHz, 110 Hz high-pass, less 350 Hz, more above 3.5 kHz.

### heartbeat
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-single-sound (heartbeat.mp3_.flac)
- Author: Independent.nu (submitted by qubodup). Licence: CC0.
- Changed: mono, trimmed to one lub-dub and shortened tail, 26 Hz high-pass, a little saturated midrange added.

### eyes-open
